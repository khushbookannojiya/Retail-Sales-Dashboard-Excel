# 📊 Retail Sales Dashboard in Microsoft Excel

An interactive Retail Sales Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPIs, Conditional Formatting, and Dashboard Design techniques.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This dashboard analyzes retail sales performance across different product categories, customer segments, regions, and shipping modes. It provides business insights through interactive visualizations and executive-level KPIs.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Help business managers answer questions like:

- Which category generates the highest sales?
- Which region is the most profitable?
- What are the top 10 selling products?
- Which customer segment contributes the most revenue?
- How do monthly sales and profit compare?

---

## 📈 Dashboard KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Profit Margin
- Average Order Value
- Average Delivery Days

---

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Region
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Sales by Segment
- Sales vs Profit

---

## 🛠 Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- GETPIVOTDATA
- Custom Number Formatting
- Conditional Formatting
- Dashboard Design

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Technology generated the highest sales.
- West region achieved the highest profit.
- Consumer customers contributed over 50% of total sales.
- Standard Class was the most preferred shipping mode.
- Monthly sales and profit showed a positive growth trend.
